What this is

An isolated atproto network: its own PDS, PLC directory, relay and Jetstream. Its accounts live in its own directory, not the public one, so nothing here reaches Bluesky or the rest of the public atproto network, and they cannot find anything here.

Apps point at it to test against real protocol services without touching the shared network that Bluesky and everyone else use.
