Demo only. Everything on this network is test data and may be lost at any time.

What this is

A sealed atproto network: its own account server, directory, relay and feed. It has no route to the public internet, and its accounts live in its own directory, not the public one, so nothing here reaches Bluesky and Bluesky cannot find anything here. Apps point at it to test against real protocol services without touching real people's data.
